The Project

Ecommerce assets
built around
trust and relief.

ReliveX needed an ecommerce-ready visual system that could carry the brand from product packaging to online sales touchpoints. The work brought together the core brand look, product presentation, and supporting collateral for the customer journey.

The project includes logo direction, packaging, print pieces, Shopify website images, and creative ads. Together, those assets give the brand a consistent presence across the product box, user guide, brochure, community content, and website.

The direction uses clean health-tech visuals, a calm mint-green palette, product mockups, and direct benefit-led imagery to support buyer confidence for a pain-relief ecommerce offer.
