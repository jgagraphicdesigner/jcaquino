The Project

From book
to listening
experience.

Aus Property Professionals had a strong educational asset in the Positively Geared book. We transformed it into an audiobook app so prospects could consume the content in a more convenient way while keeping the brand experience polished and easy to access.

The finished experience works as both a web app and a mobile app-style experience, with offline listening support so users can download the chapters and keep learning without needing an active connection.

The strategic role was simple: make the book easier to engage with, then use that value as a lead magnet to generate more qualified conversations for the client.
