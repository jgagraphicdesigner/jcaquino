The Project

A strategic
property
brand system.

Prop Moves needed a visual identity that could communicate premium property strategy, data-led confidence, and practical buyer-agency trust. The direction uses the chess-piece idea as a signal for smart movement and calculated decisions.

The approved website pack now shown here is intentionally focused: brand frames, logo exports, brochure visuals, website page captures, social media, banner, billboard, and the brochure PDF. Nothing outside the approved website folder is used for the public media on this page.

The result is a concise case study that keeps the JGA Studios page style while letting the Prop Moves deliverables show as the work itself.
