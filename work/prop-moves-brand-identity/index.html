The Project

Data-backed
property
brand build.

Prop Moves needed more than a logo. The brand had to feel strategic, premium, and investment-focused while supporting a full sales ecosystem for property investors across Australia.

JGA built the visual identity around two ideas: computer-chip precision for data-led decision making and chess-knight strategy for calculated movement. That identity then carried into the brand book, website visuals, brochure system, due diligence checklist, social proof templates, letterhead, and roll-up banner assets.

The finished system gives Prop Moves one consistent visual language for trust-building: sharp enough for data and analytics, polished enough for high-ticket property conversations, and practical enough to use across digital and print.

Mission

To merge data-driven insights with meticulous human due diligence so clients can make informed property-investment decisions, identify prime opportunities across Australia, and pursue stronger investment outcomes with integrity and transparency.

Vision

To set the standard for investment excellence by combining market analysis, risk assessment, and human review into a clear decision-making process that helps investors build enduring financial prosperity.

Brochure System

Wealth Creation
Plan
presentation.

The brochure explains how Prop Moves evolved from software-led buyer-agent mentoring into an end-to-end buyers agency. It positions the brand around proprietary data, human due diligence, and a process designed to help clients select stronger property assets.

Inside the brochure, the offer is structured around strategy options such as growing a portfolio, SMSF investing, debt reduction, upgrading, and rentvesting. It then walks through asset selection, service delivery, algorithmic filtering, street-level analysis, due diligence checks, approvals, post-purchase support, pricing, and client case studies.

Key Content Captured

  • Algorithm story
    13 years of property data and over 1,500 purchases are used as credibility anchors in the brochure narrative.
  • Filtering model
    The process moves from 15,304 suburbs to 1,520 ranked suburbs and 252 approved suburbs after human vetting.
  • Property checks
    Weekly opportunities are filtered by land, street quality, due diligence, ROI fit, finance, inspections, and client brief matching.
  • Support system
    The brochure covers legal, building and pest, property management, portfolio reviews, webinars, and ongoing newsletters.
