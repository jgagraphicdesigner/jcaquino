The Project

Data-driven
real estate
identity.

Prop Moves needed a brand identity that could feel sharp, confident, and investment-focused. The direction centers on a clean visual system that speaks to data-backed property decisions and return on investment in Australia.

The logo concept combines the precision and power of a computer chip with the strategic thinking of a chess knight. Together, those cues position Prop Moves as an innovative and calculated real estate investment partner.

The final identity uses a minimal candlelight, black, and white palette with bold Nexa typography, creating a recognizable visual system that can stand apart from competitors.
