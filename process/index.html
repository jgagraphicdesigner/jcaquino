FAQs

Common
questions

How long does a typical project take?
Ad creative projects: 3–7 days. GHL setups: 1–3 weeks. App development: 3–8 weeks depending on scope. We'll give you a specific timeline in the proposal before we start.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes — most of my clients are based in Australia, the US, and the UK. We work fully remote and am available for calls in most time zones. Payments accepted in USD, AUD, or PHP.
How many revisions are included?
It depends on the package. Starter packages include 1–2 revision rounds. Growth and retainer packages include unlimited revisions. Revisions outside scope are billed at an hourly rate.
Do you require a deposit?
Yes — 50% upfront before work begins, 50% on delivery. For retainer clients, billing is monthly in advance. This protects both parties and keeps the project moving without delays.
Can We add services later?
Absolutely. Many clients start with ad creative and later add GHL funnels or app development. We'm set up to grow with your business — the more context We have, the better the work gets.
